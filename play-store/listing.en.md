# Play Store listing — English draft

**Title:** Hindu Calendar Panchang

**Short description:** Offline Panchang, festivals, dates and personal reminders for your city.

**Full description:**

Explore a clear Hindu calendar alongside Gregorian dates. Choose an Indian city to see locally calculated Panchang details, including tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sunrise and selected daily time periods. Browse festivals and vrats, switch between month, week and today views, and keep private notes and reminders on your phone.

The app works without an account or GPS permission. Choose Hindi or English, change the lunar month naming system, and adjust text size and calendar layout. Local notification alerts are optional and depend on Android permission and device settings.

Panchang and observance dates can vary by city, tradition and calculation source. Festival coverage is not exhaustive. Government holiday notices are shown only when official year-specific data is available; a religious festival is not automatically a public holiday.
